Michal Chudoba

PhD candidate and research fellow · University of Stavanger

I work on multimodal language models and on cheaper ways to adapt them. Instead of editing weights, I train a single image that the model reads as part of its prompt: a small, portable adapter that leaves the model and the serving stack untouched.

I have also applied the idea to drilling and well data, where learned images stand in for an ontology and a heavy agent toolkit. Before Stavanger I worked on recommender systems and LLMs at Seznam.cz, and studied computer science at Charles University in Prague.
