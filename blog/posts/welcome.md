# Welcome to My Blog

Hello and welcome to my personal blog! I'm excited to share this space with you where I'll be discussing various topics related to computer science, artificial intelligence, and my journey in academia.

## About This Blog

This blog serves as a platform for me to:

- **Share insights** from my research in AI and machine learning
- **Document my academic journey** as a doctoral student
- **Discuss technical topics** that I find interesting
- **Connect with fellow researchers** and students

## What You Can Expect

I plan to write about a variety of topics, including:

### Research & Academia

- Updates on my doctoral research at the University of Stavanger
- Insights from conferences and academic events
- Thoughts on the current state of AI research

### Technical Content

- Programming tutorials and tips
- Machine learning algorithms and implementations
- Tools and technologies I use in my research

### Personal Reflections

- My journey from Prague to Stavanger
- Challenges and successes in academic life
- Thoughts on the intersection of technology and society

I'm currently pursuing my doctoral degree at the University of Stavanger, having completed my bachelor's and master's degrees in Computer Science at Charles University in Prague. My research focuses on AI and machine learning, with particular interest in:

- Machine learning algorithms
- Data analysis techniques
- Applications of AI in academic research
